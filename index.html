About

Paper Tales of Japan retells Japan's myths and folktales for curious minds around the world. Our guide, Are, is named after Hieda no Are, who memorized the tales that became the Kojiki.

Sources. Each myth episode follows the Kojiki (712) or the Nihon Shoki (720). We note the source in every video description.

How it's made. Stories are researched, written, and directed by Masa Arakaki. Illustrations, some animation, and the narration voices are created with AI tools, then edited and assembled by hand.

日本の神話と昔話を、紙細工のアニメと英語の語りで世界へ。古事記・日本書紀にもとづき、絵と声の一部はAIツールで制作しています。
